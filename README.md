## Featured Projects

### FinTrack

A financial management backend application built with Java and Spring Boot.

The project focuses on scalable backend architecture and includes:

* Budget management

* Account management

* Transactions

* Categories

* Role and permission management

* Audit logging

* Custom Java annotations

* Pagination

* UUID-based identifiers

* API response wrappers

* Database optimization

* High-load design considerations

***

### Elevora HR

An HR and workforce development platform designed to manage organizational structure, employees, skills, training, and professional development.

Key capabilities include:

* Employee management

* Departments and organizational structure

* Roles and reporting relationships

* Employee skill tracking

* Proficiency requirements

* Training requests

* Tutor management

* Training sessions

* Assessments

* Certifications

* Notifications

* Administrative dashboards

***

### Ovey Store

An e-commerce and customer savings platform for electronics retail.

Key features include:

* Product and inventory management

* Customer savings

* Order processing

* Payment verification

* Transaction tracking

* Administrative dashboards

* Search and filtering

* Pagination

* CSV and PDF exports

* Cloud-based media storage

***

### NextGen Starters

A technology education platform supporting digital skills training and AI-focused learning programs.

The platform supports programs such as:

* Software development

* AI foundations

* AI automation

* Prompt engineering

* Data analytics

* Waitlist management

* Google Sheets

### Healthcare Connect

A digital patient intake MVP designed for rural healthcare facilities.

The system is designed to improve the way patient information is collected and accessed before appointments.

Core features include:

* Digital patient registration

* Patient information review

* Digital intake submission

* Medication list management

* Staff access to patient information

* Pre-populated patient data for healthcare staff
